Characteristics of Human Voice Variation

  • Vocal Fold Micro-Tremors: Small, continuous fluctuations in pitch (jitter) and loudness (shimmer) caused by natural muscle tremors and variations in lung pressure.
  • Resonator Flexibility: Instantaneous shifts in acoustic resonance as the throat, mouth, and nasal cavities continuously change shape during speech.
  • Emotional State & Energy: Subtle changes in voice quality, tension, and tempo driven by real-time feelings, physical fatigue, or excitement.
  • Physical Coarticulation: Seamless overlapping of speech sounds driven by the physical momentum of moving lips, tongue, and jaw.
  • Involuntary Biological Noise: Subtle extra sounds integrated into speech, such as breath intakes, lip smacks, and vocal fry.
